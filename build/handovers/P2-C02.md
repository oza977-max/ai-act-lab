## Retrospective
The first draft passed its own tests, and the tests were again the weak point: they checked that headings appeared, not that rules arrived. Each review pass sharpened a test rather than the prompt. The one real-model run was the most instructive part: the untrained model's mistakes (text "null", loops, copying the example) are exactly what the bake-off and fine-tuning exist to measure and fix.

Claude drafts `docs/guideline/labelling-guideline.md`, the rulebook for labelling every gold case. The owner reviews and approves v1. Claude about 2 hours; owner about 1 hour.

## Owner decisions taken before drafting (2026-10-27)
- **D-LS-1 Missing facts.** Assume the ordinary case: the system is used in the EU and works as described. Send a case to review (`missing-fact`) only when the unstated fact would change the tier and no ordinary assumption settles it. The reason sentence states any assumption that mattered.
- **D-LS-2 Article 6(3).** An Annex III system stays high-risk unless the description clearly states a narrow, preparatory or otherwise 6(3) task, and no profiling of people. Profiling always keeps it high-risk.

- **D-LS-3 (after review pass 4).** Silence on profiling passes the "clearly stated" test: a plainly narrow task with nothing suggesting an output about an individual qualifies; no explicit denial needed.
- **D-LS-4 (after review pass 4).** Whether the Annex III 5(b) fraud-detection exclusion is judged by purpose or effect stays `unsettled-law` until guidance exists.

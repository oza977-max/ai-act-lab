Overall confidence: Flagged, then resolved. Five divergences went to the owner as the method requires. Convergence elsewhere shows stability between two passes of the same model family, not correctness; the owner's approval is the correctness check.

One lesson from this round. The largest divergence (Lee et al.) came from the primary pass not knowing who the authors were and scoring that ignorance as low authority. The independent pass looked them up. Unknown is not the same as weak; the fix is to verify before scoring, which the independent pass did.

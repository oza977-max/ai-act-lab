## 1. What changed

First run. Settings changed once before it: batch size 4 ran out of GPU memory, as did batch size 1 alone. Batch size 1 with gradient checkpointing worked (test peaks: 7.1 GB with 16 layers, 6.4 GB with 8). The owner chose 8 layers.

## 2. What we expected

Validation loss falling quickly then levelling off around 0.15 to 0.25 (the article lists in this data are noisy); held-out label accuracy well above the untrained 5 of 20.

The final model over-uses "transparency-only" (7 answers for 5 such cases).

## 4. What I learned and what I would try next

- **Loss is not the result.** At step 200 the validation loss looked good (0.176) yet no answer was usable. Only reading the actual answers showed it. Our reading list warned of this (validation loss alone misleads).
- **Noisy targets teach bad habits.** The benchmark's long, inconsistent article lists taught the model to list articles without stopping. Next try: cap targets at the few articles that decide the tier, or drop articles from practice targets; the real gold set will have short, checked citations.
- **Memory is the real constraint on 8 GB.** The borrowed 3.9 GB estimate did not hold for this model; plan training at batch size 1 with gradient checkpointing.
